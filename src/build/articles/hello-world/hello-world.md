# Introduction

Hey there, I'm Penthusiast, and this is ExploreScratch. ExploreScratch is a website I've been working on for a couple months. It's an informational and news site made for [Scratch](https://scratch.mit.edu/). 

# Why This Exists

I've been on Scratch for several years now, and I have only seen the explore page quality decrease. This isn't meant to criticize the projects that *do* make it on there, and I'm sure they are quite cool. However I enjoy coming to Scratch to look at creative and interesting projects as well as publish my own, and I didn't feel that the explore page was showing me that.

Originally I set out to solve this by creating my own explore page. It was known as ScratchExplore (confusing, considering this site is ExploreScratch), and overall it worked quite well. I always wanted to merge this with a larger site with a broader goal. Eventually I considered making a news site for Scratch, where Scratchers could write articles about happenings in the Scratch community.

In addition to news articles, I planned to help give creative quality projects the attention they deserve, and host other Scratch related tools. This idea was the beginning of ExploreScratch.

# Vision

The vision for ExploreScratch is to provide a place where Scratchers can come for news and to discover new projects and other Scratchers who are creating quality content. In the future, I plan on adding other Scratch related tools. You can have a say in this by suggesting features on my Scratch profile!
